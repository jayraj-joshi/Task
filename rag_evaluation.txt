QUERY 5: What is "Scaled Dot-Product Attention" and how is it calculated?
EXPECTED ANSWER: Scaled Dot-Product Attention is a particular attention mechanism where the input consists of queries (Q) and keys (K) of dimension dk, and values (V) of dimension dv. It is calculated by taking the dot product of the query with all keys, dividing by sqrt(dk), and applying a softmax function to obtain weights on the values: Attention(Q, K, V) = softmax(QK^T / sqrt(dk))V.

QUERY 9: What are the advantages of self-attention layers over recurrent layers in terms of computational complexity?
EXPECTED ANSWER: Self-attention layers connect all positions with a constant number of sequentially executed operations (O(1)), whereas recurrent layers require O(n) sequential operations. This makes self-attention faster when the sequence length n is smaller than the representation dimensionality d.
